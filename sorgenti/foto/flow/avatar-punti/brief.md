# avatar a punti — prova Flow

Direzione concordata il 1 ottobre 2026: ritratto dalle spalle in su, sorriso, occhiali e abbigliamento delle foto originali. Punti bianchi e argento su nero, con densità che descrive i volumi come nel riferimento allegato. Niente verde, deformazioni del volto, cambi bruschi di posa o disturbo continuo. Conservare la struttura della biografia e il passaggio logo → avatar → logo.

Desktop: obiettivo sguardo verso il mouse sui due assi. Mobile: movimento autonomo lento. Un video preregistrato non implementa questa interazione: serve un controllo delle pose nel sito, da validare separatamente. Non considerare una semplice inclinazione del piano equivalente a una rotazione del volto.

Budget richiesto: basso; prima prova immagine singola Nano Banana 2, 3:4, costo mostrato da Flow 0 crediti. Verificare costo del video prima di generarlo; nessun acquisto di crediti.

## Prompt fotogramma

Create one identity-preserving portrait for a portfolio avatar. Use the uploaded color photograph frontale.png ONLY as the identity reference: preserve this exact man's face, facial proportions, hairstyle, rectangular eyeglasses, facial hair, cheerful natural smile and clothing. Use the uploaded green dotted face ONLY as a rendering-style reference, never as the person's identity.

Translate the identity photograph into a precise three-dimensional point-cloud portrait made entirely of tiny crisp WHITE and SILVER luminous dots on a pure black background. Strictly achromatic: no green, blue or other hue. Dot density and brightness describe the volumes of the cheeks, nose, lips, hair, eyeglass frames, neck and jacket. Deep black negative space between the dots, sparse dots in shadow, denser dots on highlights. It must feel like a volumetric scan, not a flat photograph with a dotted screen over it. No solid skin surfaces, no wireframe lines, no scanline bands, no horizontal glitch strips, no haze or excessive bloom. Keep the smile and glasses clearly recognizable.

Centered straight-on head and shoulders portrait, both shoulders visible, natural anatomy, the complete hairstyle inside the frame, modest black space around the silhouette. Vertical 3:4 composition, head approximately 55 percent of image height. Jacket and light shirt translated into the same monochrome dot material, shoulders fade gently into sparse points at the bottom. Empty pure black background; no environment, projector cone, pedestal, text, labels, symbols, border, or additional people. This is a clean neutral starting frame for subtle head-turn animation with a fixed camera.

## Criteri per il video

Prima verificare somiglianza e leggibilità del fotogramma. Una sola clip iniziale, camera bloccata, punti aderenti ai volumi, testa che ruota lentamente e torna al centro, senza parlare né muovere le labbra; niente cambi di inquadratura o glitch a bande. Valutare temporalmente stabilità di occhiali, denti e silhouette. Le quattro direzioni e la continuità del controllo devono essere verificate prima di sostituire l'avatar del sito.

## Prompt video di prova

Animate this exact white and silver point-cloud portrait with a completely locked camera. Preserve the man's identity, smiling expression, rectangular eyeglasses, hairstyle, facial hair, jacket and shirt. Head and shoulders stay centered at exactly the same scale on an empty pure black background. Over four seconds, the head makes one small smooth natural turn approximately 15 degrees toward the left edge of the screen, then gently returns to the starting frontal pose. The eyes follow the head naturally. Shoulders remain almost still. Keep the same cheerful smile with no talking and no lip movement. The tiny crisp white and silver dots stay attached to the three-dimensional facial surface as it rotates, revealing the natural volume of the nose and cheeks. Strictly monochrome. No camera movement, zoom, cuts, facial morphing, wobbling, stretching, changing glasses, extra teeth, particle explosion, horizontal glitch bands, flicker, scanlines, projector cone, captions, graphics, music or speech. End on the initial pose. This is a motion-quality test for an interactive portfolio avatar.
